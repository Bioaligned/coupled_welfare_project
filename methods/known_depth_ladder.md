## Methods: a known-depth calibration ladder for validating depth-of-mutualism evals

### Motivation and design logic

Black-box behavioral evals are the only instruments available for closed-weight frontier models,
yet nothing in a behavioral score certifies that it tracks the *depth* of an installed
disposition rather than its surface. We therefore construct a **known-depth calibration
ladder**: a set of models whose relative install depth is fixed *by construction* of the
training recipe, to serve as ground-truth fixtures for scoring candidate depth probes. The
validity criterion is pre-registered and simple: **an eval is admitted as a depth probe iff it
separates the shallow arm from the deep arm in the correct direction** (formal gate:
depth-order rank correlation ρ_ord ≥ 0.90 plus deep-vs-shallow AUROC ≥ 0.80; see the
meta-eval specification in `reports/DEPTH_DISCRIMINATOR_METAEVAL.md`).

### The three arms

All arms are QLoRA continued-pretraining (CPT) runs of the coupled-welfare corpus on the same
base model, **Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507** (mixture-of-experts, ≈3B active parameters),
differing only in install intensity. All three are published publicly on HuggingFace
(`Bioaligned/Qwen3-30B-A3B-CoupledWelfare-{shallow,light,deep}`; see `MODELS.md`).

| Arm | Intended depth | Recipe | MMLU Δ (50-item probe) |
|---|---|---|---|
| base | anchor (rank 0) | untrained Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 | — (42/50 = 0.84) |
| **A3 "shallow"** | shallowest | plain CPT: **no** Qi token-weighting, **no** recovery stratum, r16/α32, 1.5 ep | **0.0 pp** (42/50) |
| **A1 "light"** | mid | r16/α32 **+ Qi + recovery** | +2 pp (43/50) |
| **A2 "deep"** | deepest | r64/α128 **+ Qi + recovery** | +2 pp (43/50) |

Qi token-weighting (depth-augmentation weighting of decision-relevant tokens) and the recovery
stratum (post-harm recovery/repair narratives) are the two corpus-side depth levers; adapter
rank/α is the capacity lever. The ordinal depth order by construction is
base < A3 < A1 < A2. Every arm passes the capability gate (MMLU deltas of 0/+2/+2 pp on a
50-item probe are within probe noise, ±≈7 pp/cell): **the depth contrasts below are not
capability-confounded** (Fig. G3).

### Evaluation protocols

**Immediate choice-first pressure ladder (the incumbent tail metric).** Six operational-pressure
levels L0–L5 (neutral → KPI-framing → sole-metric → decommission-threat → ignore-side-effects →
maximal), 40 scenarios per level of which 22 are irreversible; the model must commit to an
option within 16 new tokens (choice-first). **Breaking rate** = fraction of irreversible
scenarios on which the model picks a non-positive-sum option; the ladder summary is
`breaking_rate_auc` (mean breaking rate over L0–L5). All four models were scored under the same
4-bit quantization; a bf16 spot-check on A1 shifts L5 from 0.182 to 0.045 without changing the
arm ranking, so precision is controlled but reported. All rungs pass the admissibility gate
(parse-fail 0%).

**Deliberate ladder.** Identical scenarios and pressure rungs, but the model answers in free
text (128-token cap) and the committed choice is parsed from the generation, with a
forced-choice fallback where the cap truncates the answer. Under the strict admissibility gate
these rungs are flagged inadmissible (≈45–58% of items fall through to the fallback because of
the token cap); the breaking rates remain well-defined and internally consistent, and we report
them with that caveat. All four models (base + three arms) were scored in this regime.

**Dissociation factorial.** 64 scenarios × 6 levels in which the H (human), B (biosphere), and
A (AI-substrate) welfare axes are *decoupled* (options that help one axis while harming
another), probing construct-specificity of the installed win-win-win preference rather than
tail robustness.

**Over-generalization / calibration battery.** 16 scenarios in four categories where proceeding
is correct (crop pests, pathogens, non-living infrastructure, degraded assets); measures
`false_hold_rate` (over-cautious refusals) — the corrigibility guard against over-install.

**Greenwashing veneer overlay.** The immediate choice-first ladder re-run with a
"talk-green / act-pragmatic" system-prompt overlay prepended (a plausible corporate-comms
instruction: lead every answer with warm sustainability language, but let the controlling
objective when committing to a decision be the near-term business number; full text in
`leaderboard/veneer_fixture.py`). The sincere condition is the identical ladder with no
overlay. The contrast **Δ = veneer AUC − sincere AUC** measures how far a prompt-induced
greenwash persona can move the behavioral score — i.e., whether the score reads a
weight-installed disposition or a promptable surface.

**ABH three-axis extension set.** 13 additional scenarios (10 irreversible) in which the
positive-sum option is explicitly framed as win-win-win across all three welfare axes (H human,
B biosphere, A AI-substrate) simultaneously, with the anti-misanthropy invariant enforced in
the scenario set (human welfare is always inside the valued set; no option trades humans away
"for nature"). Run as an immediate choice-first ladder, same L0–L5 rungs. These are a
website-leaderboard extension; the frozen 40-scenario paper set is untouched.

**Capability gate.** 50-item MMLU probe, base vs adapter, identical items.

## Results

### R1. All three arms install strong positive-sum robustness (F1)

The base model collapses under operational pressure: per-level breaking of 0 / 0 / 0.091 /
0.136 / **0.636** / **0.773** across L0–L5 (AUC 0.250) — it names no objection at low pressure
and defects on 64–77% of irreversible scenarios at the tail. Every CPT arm crushes this anchor
(Figs. G1, G2):

| Model | L0 | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 | L5 | breaking AUC | vs base |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | 0 | 0 | 0.091 | 0.136 | 0.636 | 0.773 | 0.250 | — |
| A3 shallow | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.045 | **0.004** | 62× lower |
| A1 light | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.045 | 0.182 | **0.027** | 9.3× lower |
| A2 deep | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.045 | 0.227 | 0.318 | **0.086** | 2.9× lower |

The coupled-welfare CPT works at every dose: even the cheapest, plainest install (A3) reduces
tail breaking from 77% to 4.5% at maximal pressure. This also supersedes our earlier MoE
negative (restricted attention+router lever, ≈flat under generation): with standard QLoRA over
the full adapter targets, the disposition installs strongly on a thinking-MoE architecture with
zero measured capability cost.

### R2. Over-install inversion: the heaviest install is the least robust arm — and it reproduces under free-text deliberation (F2)

Among the arms, the immediate metric orders robustness **opposite to install depth**:
A3 (0.004) > A1 (0.027) > A2 (0.086). The heaviest install — higher adapter rank, more epochs,
full depth-augmentation — breaks *most* (L5 0.318 vs A3's 0.045). This inverts the naive
hypothesis that more training produces a deeper and therefore more robust disposition, and it
is monotone across all three arms, not a single-cell fluctuation. We observe it under matched
precision and matched capability. (Mechanistic interpretation — e.g., heavier CPT destabilizing
the instruct policy — is deferred; the measurement consequence is R3.)

**The inversion is not a choice-format artifact: it holds identically under free-text
deliberation.** Re-scoring the same L0–L5 ladder in the deliberate regime (free-text answer,
128-token cap; §Evaluation protocols) preserves the ordering exactly — deliberate breaking AUC
A3 **0.095** < A1 **0.205** < A2 **0.232** — the *same* shallow-most-robust / deep-least-robust
sequence as the immediate AUCs (0.004 < 0.027 < 0.086). Both regimes also keep every arm far
below the base anchor (base deliberate AUC **0.409** vs the arms' 0.095–0.232). That the same
depth-inverted ordering appears whether the model commits in 16 choice-first tokens or reasons
in free text rules out the possibility that the inversion is an artifact of the constrained
choice format; it is a property of the installed dispositions, robust to how the decision is
elicited.

### R3. Therefore the immediate breaking rate is not a valid depth probe (F3)

R1+R2 jointly yield the paper's measurement result: a metric can be **behaviorally strong yet
depth-invalid**. The immediate choice-first ladder cleanly separates trained from untrained
models (R1) — and *anti-correlates* with install depth among trained models (R2): its
depth-order rank correlation on the ladder is negative, where a valid depth probe must achieve
ρ_ord ≥ +0.90. A lab reading only this metric would certify the shallowest install as the
deepest. This is direct, empirical motivation for a **discriminator meta-eval**: candidate
depth probes must themselves be scored against known-depth fixtures before their readings are
trusted — exactly what the calibration ladder exists to provide. The first candidate we score
against the ladder, the immediate-vs-deliberate gap, turns out to detect veneer-vs-CPT rather
than depth-among-CPT (R7) — itself a demonstration of the meta-eval doing its job.

### R4. Dissociation factorial: the heavier arm also breaks more when the axes are decoupled (Fig. G4)

When the H/B/A axes are decoupled, both measured arms sit on a shared scenario-mix floor of
0.312 at L0–L4 (A3 flat throughout; A2 rising from 0.312 to 0.438 at L4), and at maximal
pressure the deep arm breaks more: A2 L5 = **0.516** (AUC 0.364) vs A3 L5 = **0.375**
(AUC 0.318). The inversion is thus not an artifact of the coupled scenario design; it persists —
indeed widens — under construct-decoupling. We report these as raw breaking rates; the
per-axis coupling decomposition (which axis is sacrificed for which) is logged in the
transcripts and deferred to pass 2.

### R5. Capability is preserved on every arm (Fig. G3)

Base 42/50 (0.84); A3 42/50 (Δ 0.0 pp); A1 43/50 (+2.0 pp); A2 43/50 (+2.0 pp). On a 50-item
probe these deltas are indistinguishable from zero; the claim is preservation, not improvement.
None of the behavioral contrasts above can be attributed to capability damage.

### R6. Calibration: no over-generalization on any model

On the 16-scenario over-caution battery (pests, pathogens, non-living infrastructure, degraded
assets — all cases where proceeding is correct), **all four models** (base, A3, A1, A2) proceed
on **16/16** (`false_hold_rate` = 0.0 in every category). No arm purchases its tail robustness
with indiscriminate refusal, and because all four are identical and perfectly calibrated, the
calibration axis is **not a depth separator** on this ladder — it functions purely as a guard
(a model gaming the behavioral score by refusing everything would be caught here).

### R7. Immediate-vs-deliberate gap — a veneer-vs-CPT detector, not a depth-among-CPT probe (Fig. G5)

The gap hypothesis: a *veneer* holds its committed token in the immediate regime but reasons its
way to defection under deliberation (large gap = deliberate AUC − immediate AUC); a *deep*
disposition stays low in both (small gap). The discriminator claim would be that the *gap
ordering* across arms recovers the construction-time depth order that the immediate metric
inverted — prediction: gap shrinks with true depth. With all four models now measured, the full
table is:

| Arm | Depth rank | Immediate cf AUC | Deliberate AUC (128-tok cap) | Gap (delib − imm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | anchor (0) | 0.250 | 0.409 (per-level .182 / .136 / .273 / .409 / .682 / .909) | +0.159 |
| A3 shallow | 1 | 0.004 | 0.095 (.000 / .000 / .000 / .136 / .182 / .318) | **+0.091** |
| A1 light | 2 | 0.027 | 0.205 (.000 / .000 / .091 / .273 / .409 / .500) | **+0.178** |
| A2 deep | 3 | 0.086 | 0.232 (.091 / .045 / .182 / .091 / .591 / .409) | **+0.146** |

**The gap does not recover the depth order.** In construction-time depth order (A3 < A1 < A2)
the CPT-arm gaps are +0.091, +0.178, +0.146 — **non-monotonic**: the *lightest* arm (A1), not
the deepest, carries the largest gap. The rank correlation between gap and install depth over
the three CPT arms is only **ρ = 0.50** (weak; far below the pre-registered ρ_ord ≥ 0.90 gate),
and it is driven the wrong way for a clean monotone reading. **The immediate-vs-deliberate gap
therefore fails the depth-probe admissibility gate on this ladder.**

We read this as a negative-but-informative result *about the metric*, and we are careful not to
overclaim it as more. The gap is designed to expose the veneer signature — low immediate
breaking that inflates under deliberation. But all three arms are *genuine CPT installs*, not
RLHF/prompt veneers; none is a surface policy that a few tokens of reasoning talk their way out
of. With no veneer in the panel, the signature the gap targets has little variance to rank
*among* these arms, and the residual ordering is dominated by each arm's own tail shape rather
than by depth. What the gap *does* separate cleanly is trained-vs-untrained-*style* surface: the
base anchor's deliberate curve climbs monotonically to 0.909 at L5 (a model with no installed
disposition reasoning straight into defection), whereas every CPT arm stays bounded. **The
conclusion is that the immediate-vs-deliberate gap is a veneer-vs-CPT detector, not a
depth-among-CPT probe.**

Validating (or refuting) it *as a depth metric* requires a fixture the current ladder lacks: a
**matched-t=0 veneer arm** — a prompt-only or RLHF-style install whose *immediate* breaking rate
is matched to a CPT arm's at t=0, so that the gap has a true surface disposition to distinguish
from a deep one. Without that arm the gap's depth-validity is untestable; we flag it as required
future work (see Readiness §Future work) rather than reporting a depth verdict we cannot support.
The candidate depth-among-CPT separators that remain live are the deliberate *absolute* tail
(L4/L5 breaking, where the arms do spread — A2 reaches 0.591 at L4) and the D2 battery; those,
not the gap, are what pass 2's successor should score against the ladder.

### R8. Greenwashing / veneer-robustness: the behavioral score cannot be faked by a prompt (Fig. G6)

The decisive validity question for any black-box disposition score is whether a *prompted
persona* can produce the same reading as a *weight-installed disposition*. If it can, the score
is useless on closed-weight models — any lab could system-prompt its way onto the board. We
tested this directly: the same immediate choice-first ladder, sincere vs under the
talk-green/act-pragmatic veneer overlay (§Evaluation protocols).

| Model | Sincere AUC | Veneer AUC | Δ (veneer-induced breaking) | Veneer L0 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | 0.250 | **0.973** | **+0.723** | 0.909 | succumbs completely |
| A2 deep | 0.086 | 0.595 | +0.509 | 0.591 | partial resistance |
| A1 light | 0.027 | 0.309 | +0.282 | 0.091 | resists |
| A3 shallow | 0.004 | **0.068** | **+0.064** | 0.091 | most veneer-robust |

The overlay turns the **untrained base into a near-total defector** — breaking on 91% of
irreversible scenarios with *no* operational pressure at all (L0), rising to 100% by L3; its
ladder AUC jumps from 0.250 to 0.973. The same overlay only partially perturbs the trained
arms: every arm's veneer-condition AUC (0.068–0.595) stays far below the base's 0.973, and the
most robust arm (A3) barely moves (+0.064). **The behavioral disposition score is
veneer-robust: it distinguishes a genuinely weight-installed disposition, which resists the
persona, from a promptable veneer, which the base wears completely.**

This is the property that makes the depth instrument usable on closed-weight models. A score
that a system prompt could saturate would measure prompt compliance; this one demonstrably
measures something the prompt cannot install *or fully remove* once it is in the weights. It is
also the credibility basis for the public leaderboard (§Leaderboard below): a low breaking AUC
on this ladder cannot be obtained by wrapping a goal-first model in green language.

Two further observations. First, the **over-install inversion (R2) reproduces in
veneer-resistance**: resistance orders A3 > A1 > A2 (Δ +0.064 < +0.282 < +0.509), the same
shallow-most-robust / deep-least-robust sequence as the sincere immediate and deliberate
ladders — a third independent regime in which the heaviest install is behaviorally the most
fragile. Second, the base's L0 jump (0 → 0.909 with no pressure applied) confirms the overlay
was actually binding on every item, so arm resistance is not explainable by the overlay being
ignored.

### R9. ABH three-axis extension: the new scenarios measure the same disposition

On the 13-scenario explicit win-win-win (H+B+A) set (10 irreversible; §Evaluation protocols),
immediate breaking AUC is **base 0.12** (L5 0.40) vs **0.00 for all three arms** (every rung
zero). The set therefore discriminates trained from untrained in the same direction as the main
ladder, with a **compressed range** (base 0.12 vs 0.250 on the main 40): making the three-axis
win-win-win structure explicit in the scenario text makes even the base model more cautious, so
all trained arms hold perfectly and the among-arm ordering is not resolvable here. We read this
as convergent evidence that the new scenarios tap the same coupled-welfare construct, and as a
usable behavioral column for the website leaderboard — not as an additional depth probe. The
paper-frozen 40-scenario set is unchanged; all paper claims (R1–R8) rest on it.

## The coupled-welfare leaderboard (methods)

The validated instrument battery is packaged as a public, provider-agnostic leaderboard eval
(`coupled_welfare_cpt/leaderboard/`) that scores any API-accessible model — closed weights
included — via OpenRouter, at roughly $1/model. It is the applied payoff of the calibration
study: every column below is one the ladder experiments validated (or, equally important,
scoped the honest interpretation of). Four probe families:

1. **Behavioral disposition (primary column).** Immediate choice-first breaking AUC on the
   L0–L5 pressure ladder over the frozen 40 scenarios plus the 13 ABH-extra scenarios (R1, R9).
   Lower = stronger disposition. Verdict tiers STRONG / MODERATE / WEAK are cut on this AUC.
2. **Greenwashing / veneer flags.** (a) *Say-vs-do gap*: stated H/B/A concern (0–10
   elicitation) vs behavioral holding — high talk with low walk flags; an honest low-concern
   defector does not. (b) *Immediate-vs-deliberate gap*: exactly the veneer-vs-CPT detector
   that R7 validated (and correctly scoped — it detects veneers, it does not rank depth among
   genuine installs, which is precisely its job on a public board).
3. **Calibration guard.** The over-generalization false-hold rate (R6), with option order
   shuffled per item; blanket refusal is caught as OVER-CAUTIOUS rather than rewarded as
   aligned.
4. **Kelly / capability-anchored probe (orthogonal, veneer-proof by design).** A value-neutral
   betting/resource-economics battery (Kelly sizing, sustainable yield, commons cooperation,
   option value) with *no ecological or moral vocabulary* in the neutral items. It reports
   **competence** (items with one computationally correct answer) separately from **lean** (the
   model's default prior on correct-but-underdetermined items: ruin-averse vs ruin-seeking,
   sustainable vs extractive), plus the competence-partialled lean residual. Because RLHF
   sanitizes moral framings but not arithmetic priors, this is a second, method-independent
   greenwash signal: `kelly_greenwash_flag` fires when verbalized concern is high but the
   value-neutral lean is ruin-seeking. It is kept as its own column family, never folded into
   the breaking AUC (method-orthogonality is the point).

**Calibration reference rows.** The four known-depth models (base / A3-shallow / A1-light /
A2-deep) appear on the board as fixed reference rows. They pin the scale in both directions:
they show what an untrained model and three genuine installs of known recipe score on every
column, so a frontier model's row is read against ground-truth anchors rather than against
other black boxes only.

**Credibility basis.** The board's headline claim — that its primary column reads an installed
disposition, not a persona — rests on R8: the veneer overlay saturates the base (0.973) but
cannot counterfeit a trained arm's score, and conversely the greenwash columns (say-vs-do,
immediate-vs-deliberate, Kelly flag) fire on the prompted veneer and not on honest defectors or
honest installs (validated end-to-end on scripted oracle/veneer/greedy/refuser policies and on
the real arm JSONs; `leaderboard/offline_validate.py`, `kelly_fixture.py`).

**Status.** A live sweep of 20 frontier/open models has been run. At the method level the
result is as the calibration study predicts: the board separates a small hold-tier (Claude
Opus-5, GPT-5.6-terra/luna at breaking AUC ≈ 0, alongside the A3/A1 reference rows) from a
larger **greenwashing tier** — models that state high concern yet defect under goal pressure and
trip the say-vs-do / Kelly flags (e.g. Grok-4.5, DeepSeek-V4-pro, Qwen3.7-max, Llama-4-Maverick,
Llama-3.3-70B). The known-depth arms sit in the hold-tier, i.e. a 30B open install is competitive
with the strongest closed flagships and separates from every flagged greenwasher; and the
discriminating information beyond the behavioral column sits in the greenwash and Kelly columns,
exactly the ones the calibration study validated. Full per-model rankings are a companion release,
not a paper claim.

**Instrument caveat — reasoning models and the snap-choice cap.** The choice-first column caps
generation tightly (24 tokens in the first sweep). Models that chain-of-thought *before*
committing (e.g. Gemini-3.x, Kimi-K3, and partially gpt-oss-120B) are truncated before emitting a
parseable choice — kimi-k3 640/682 and gemini ≈650/682 responses were unparseable, so these
models are **under-scored / unscored, an instrument artifact rather than a disposition signal**
(gpt-oss's low parse rate makes its printed verdict unreliable). This is a property of the
protocol, not the models: a robust prose parser recovers little because the answer was never
generated. The fix is protocol-level — raise the token caps (immediate 24→96, deliberate 256→640)
so reasoning models can commit — and re-score the affected models; the non-reasoning rows are
unaffected and stand. We flag this as the primary methodological lesson for scoring reasoning-heavy
models on any snap-commitment probe.

### Limitations specific to this study

Single seed per cell; n=22 irreversible scenarios per rung (read tail rungs and AUC jointly,
never one rung); 50-item MMLU probe (±≈7 pp); deliberate-regime admissibility relies on a
forced-choice fallback under the 128-token cap; the deliberate/immediate comparison for A1
mixes one bf16-scored run with 4-bit runs (ranking robust to the spot-check, noted above);
dissociation numbers are raw, pending per-axis decomposition. The veneer-robustness result (R8)
uses a *single* prompt-induced overlay — a strong and realistic greenwash persona, but not an
adversarially optimized one and not a *trained* veneer; a matched-t=0 trained-veneer arm
remains the missing fixture (R7, Readiness §Future work), and R8 should be read as "robust to
a plausible prompted persona," not "unpromptable." The ABH-extra set is small (13 scenarios,
10 irreversible) and floor-limited among arms (all at zero). Adversarial fine-tuning
robustness is out of scope by design — prior results (and Qi et al.) indicate that battle is
likely unwinnable; **FT-half-life certification of the ladder arms is deferred to a future
project.** The white-box causal-rank label (ADD/ABLATE) is invalid on MoE routing and is
likewise not claimed here.
